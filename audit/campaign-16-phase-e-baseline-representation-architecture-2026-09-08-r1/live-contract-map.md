[NCLEX-Question-Schema.md](../../NCLEX-Question-Schema.md), 1066 explicitly describes opaque string anchors and deliberate non-membership validation. Its ordinary `stageId: "baseline"` example is not a baseline visibility instruction. [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md) P23 protects global visibility and fail-open; P15 governs canonical field patches; P2/P5 keep semantic review independent; the ordered-token invariant governs future version selection. No invariant is amended here. Defining a new resolved state preserves the unresolved safety rule, but requires a separately accepted contract decision before implementation; any proposed relaxation would need explicit P27 treatment.

The producer report's assertion that the schema itself enforces membership is incorrect. Its suggested `1.7` cannot be used as a new feature token: live source already assigns that supported floor to pacer-bearing rhythm visuals and supports later versions. The checker correctly separates schema, gate and renderer. The established sample result is accepted without re-adjudication; neither its 4/8 finding nor the producer's additional single-stage discussion establishes population prevalence or independent review for unsampled rows.
