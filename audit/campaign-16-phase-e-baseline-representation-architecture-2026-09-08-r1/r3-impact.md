The falsified premise is the adequacy of the declared-stage-only answer domain. R3 §B.4–5 requires every repaired primary anchor to be a non-empty string that is byte-exactly a member of the frozen stage list. The inference in §C.4 that cardinality one makes the correct semantic choice forced is not valid for an answer domain that also contains baseline. The checker sample did not independently survey the 25 single-stage parents; do not turn this architecture finding into new dispositions for those rows. It establishes that “one declared stage” alone is insufficient semantic proof, not that every such row is wrong.

Recommend retirement/supersession by owner action, rather than a local textual amendment. The new representation changes §A's “no contract change” framing, §B's membership invariant, §C.1's allowed value, §C.2's metadata immutability, §C.4's forced-choice tier, §E.3's verifier, §E.6's review model for single-stage choices, §E.7's no-census-movement assumption if bank floors rise, and §F's contract-change stop.
