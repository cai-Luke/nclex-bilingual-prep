## Owner amendments

### A. Review-seat distribution: global with borrowing

Accept the brief's recommended global reservation model.

Ordinary Study with `Revisit missed questions` enabled must reliably recycle missed material even when the learner's Needs-review backlog is concentrated in one category.

Reserved remediation questions may therefore borrow category capacity as §7 describes.

This intentionally permits the remediation slice to distort the ordinary Study category mix.

Because current P10 binds ordinary Study to NCLEX category weighting, record the architectural exception explicitly rather than silently violating it:

* add the narrowest appropriate P10 application/amendment;
* ordinary non-remediation seats continue to follow NCLEX weighting;
* a bounded remediation reservation may deviate from that weighting based on learner Needs-review state;
* the exception exists because strict unseen-first category sampling can indefinitely suppress missed-question recurrence while the unseen bank continues expanding;
* this does not redefine strict exam simulation or authorize broader adaptive category weighting.

Do not broaden P10 beyond this forcing incident.

### B. Small-set reservation

Replace the proposed rounding rule with:

* toggle off → `R = 0`;
* empty review pool → `R = 0`;
* `N < 5` → `R = 0`;
* otherwise `R = min(reviewPoolSize, max(1, floor(N / 5)))`.

Examples:

* N=2 → 0
* N=4 → 0
* N=5 → 1
* N=10 → 2
* N=25 → 5
* N=50 → 10

The reserved remediation share therefore never exceeds the intended one-in-five proportion merely because of rounding.

Ordinary tier-1 backfill after unseen exhaustion remains allowed exactly as the brief specifies and is not counted against `R`.
